Name
WeHaveIt

Short description (132 characters max)
Stop buying what you already own. WeHaveIt reminds you on shopping sites if this product is already on your list.

Detailed description
WeHaveIt is a simple reminder for online shopping.

When you buy a cable, a pan, vitamins, or printer ink, mark it once. The next time you open that product — on Shopee, Amazon, FairPrice, Lazada, or another supported shop — a bar appears: already in your home.

How to use it
1. Open a product page.
2. Click “We have this”, or right-click the page and choose “WeHaveIt: I already bought this”.
3. Come back later. If it is the same item, WeHaveIt warns you before you pay again.

What it does
• Saves items you mark as already owned
• Shows a reminder on supported shopping product pages
• Matches the same item across shops when the titles are clearly the same
• Stores your list on this computer only

What it does not do
• It does not inject coupons or cashback
• It does not read your email or bank
• It does not upload your list to our servers in this version

Free: up to 50 saved items on this browser. Pro (sold on our website with Paddle, not in this store) removes that cap.

Does this extension collect user data?
Yes — only product details from pages the user opens or saves (title, price, image URL, page URL), stored locally in Chrome. No account, no server upload, no ads.

Permission justifications

storage
Save the user’s owned-item list on this device.

contextMenus
Let the user save the current product with a right-click.

scripting
Read the product title, price, image, and URL when the user saves a page or when the reminder bar runs on a shopping product page.

activeTab
Access the page the user is looking at when they click the extension icon.

Host permissions (shopping sites)
Show the reminder bar on known shopping product pages such as Shopee, Amazon, Lazada, and FairPrice.

Optional host permissions
Used only if the user saves a product on a shop that is not in the default list. Chrome will ask first.
